Shorts Pipeline

Shorts Pipeline is a personal tool that turns a long YouTube video into vertical 9:16 short clips: it transcribes the source, ranks the strongest moments, renders them with captions, and can upload the finished clips to the operator's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Downloads a source video the operator supplies and transcribes the speech.
  • Ranks the most clip-worthy moments and cuts them to 9:16 portrait format.
  • Burns in word-timed captions and an optional hook overlay.
  • Uploads the finished clip to the operator's own YouTube channel, at the privacy setting the operator chooses.

Why it asks for Google access

The tool connects to YouTube through Google OAuth 2.0 so it can upload clips directly to the operator's channel and display which channel is currently linked. It requests only:

  • youtube.upload — to publish a finished clip.
  • youtube.readonly — to read basic channel identity (name, handle, avatar).

It does not access Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google service, and it does not read private videos, comments, subscribers, or analytics.

Who can use it

Shorts Pipeline is not a public service. There is no sign-up, it is not distributed as a product, and it is operated solely by its author on his own private server for his own single Google account.
